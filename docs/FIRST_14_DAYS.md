# First 14 Days After Launch

## Daily during the first week

- Review new issues.
- Remove exposed secrets immediately.
- Label and prioritize reports.
- Consolidate duplicates.
- Update known issues.
- Record hardware compatibility reports.
- Confirm documentation problems are assigned.
- Avoid adding major new features until launch blockers stabilize.

## At day 7

- Publish a short beta status update.
- Summarize confirmed hardware.
- Identify the most common installation failures.
- Decide whether a beta hotfix is needed.
- Update the roadmap.

## At day 14

- Review readiness for `v1.0.0-beta.2`.
- Close invalid or abandoned reports.
- Convert repeated support questions into documentation.
- Identify beta blockers for the release candidate.
- Publish a community thank-you update.
