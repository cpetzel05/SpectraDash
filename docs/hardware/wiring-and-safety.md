# Wiring and Safety

Disconnect power before attaching or removing display cables, HATs, or adapters.

## General precautions

- Verify voltage requirements.
- Align ribbon cables correctly.
- Do not force connectors.
- Use a stable power supply.
- Avoid conductive surfaces.
- Protect the display from bending, impact, moisture, and static discharge.

## Display-specific instructions

Always follow the hardware manufacturer's documentation for the exact panel and controller revision. Similar-looking boards may use different pinouts or voltage requirements.

## First power-on

Test the vendor sample software before SpectraDash when working with new hardware. This separates hardware and driver problems from application configuration.
