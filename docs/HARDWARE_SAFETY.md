# Hardware Safety

- Use the correct Waveshare 13.3-inch e-Paper HAT+ (E).
- Confirm the panel is 1600×1200 E6 full color.
- Test the official Waveshare example before enabling SpectraDash output.
- Do not leave the panel powered in an active high-voltage state.
- The driver enters sleep after each update.
- Do not refresh more often than every 180 seconds.
- Do not bend or strain the FPC cable.
- Use a stable power supply.
