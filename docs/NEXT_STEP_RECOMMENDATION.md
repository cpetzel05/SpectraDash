# Recommended Next Step

After Sprint 4 is imported and verified, the next step should be a controlled public-beta launch rather than immediate broad promotion.

## Recommended sequence

1. Publish `v1.0.0-beta.1`.
2. Invite a small first wave of 5–10 testers.
3. Observe installation and hardware reports for 48–72 hours.
4. Fix any beta blockers.
5. Update known issues and documentation.
6. Publish the wider community announcements.
7. Begin the `v1.0.0-beta.2` milestone.

This staged approach reduces the chance that a basic installation or packaging defect becomes the first experience for a large audience.
