# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect differences in experience, language, background, and hardware access.
- Protect private information shared during support conversations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method listed in `SECURITY.md` or the repository owner profile.
