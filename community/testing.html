Public Testing

Focus on installation, stability, display synchronization, themes, units, and hardware compatibility.

  1. Perform a fresh installation.
  2. Test both primary layouts.
  3. Switch Fahrenheit and Celsius.
  4. Enable theme rotation.
  5. Verify alerts and astronomy.
  6. Run for at least 24 hours.
  7. Reboot and confirm recovery.

Include in bug reports

  • Version
  • Pi model
  • Display model
  • OS
  • Layout and theme
  • Steps and logs
